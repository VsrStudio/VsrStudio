HungerGame

HungerGame is a thrilling survival-based plugin for PaperMC. It brings the excitement of arena combat, resource gathering, and strategic gameplay to your server. Create your own Hunger Games experience and let players compete for ultimate survival.

Features

  • Customizable arenas and game settings
  • Dynamic item drops and resource gathering
  • Solo or team-based survival matches
  • Built-in matchmaking and leaderboard system
  • Optimized for seamless PaperMC server integration
