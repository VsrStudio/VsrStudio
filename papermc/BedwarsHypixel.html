BedwarsHypixel

BedwarsHypixel is a dynamic and engaging plugin for PaperMC that brings the classic Bedwars gameplay to your server. With custom features and mechanics, it allows players to experience the thrill of competitive team-based combat.

Features

  • Customizable Bedwars maps and arenas
  • Team-based PvP combat with beds to protect
  • In-depth economy and shop system
  • Real-time leaderboard tracking
  • Seamless PaperMC server integration
