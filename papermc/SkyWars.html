Sky Wars

Sky Wars is a thrilling PaperMC plugin that brings exciting PvP battles in the skies. Players fight to be the last one standing on floating islands while collecting resources and crafting strategies.

Dependencies: Slime World Manager, ProtocolLib

Features

  • Customizable maps with floating islands
  • Exciting PvP combat in the sky
  • Chest loots and random item generation
  • Real-time leaderboard for players
  • Seamless integration with PaperMC
